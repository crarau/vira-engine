Scaling Vira with $1M
=====================

The corpus is the moat, so most of the money goes into widening it — more
categories, more platforms, and a conversion feedback loop that records which
generated ads actually sold rather than which source videos merely performed.
That turns the evidence gate from a fixed threshold into something that gets
stricter with every ad shipped, which is the only version of this business that
compounds instead of just scaling headcount-free.
