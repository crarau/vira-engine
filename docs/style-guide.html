Forensic Teal
A cleaner technical palette pairing warm greige neutrals with assertive deep teal and copper editorial highlights.

Colors

Deep teal suggests measured intelligence and trust without falling into generic enterprise blue. The warm-biased neutrals keep the product human and readable, while copper and muted moss create a strong but sophisticated visual language for unsupported versus sourced claims.
